# Monthly close workflow

1. Identify the fiscal year and period.
2. Inventory the period inputs without modifying originals.
3. Reconcile bank, sales, purchases, payroll, and material contracts using the relevant skills.
4. Record missing evidence and unresolved differences in the task workspace.
5. Run deterministic validation or calculators only when a standard task template exists.
6. Produce a draft close memo; human approval remains external to `acctx`.
