# Controlled export workflow

1. Confirm that the task workspace contains the intended inputs, calculations, drafts, and checklist.
2. Generate an evidence index and review its warnings, file list, sizes, and SHA-256 values.
3. Choose the smallest necessary bundle scope; company profile and year inputs may contain sensitive information.
4. Export a task, audit, or tax bundle. All bundles remain draft and unsubmitted.
5. Verify the resulting ZIP with `acctx export verify`.
6. Record the Bundle ID and archive SHA-256 in the task checklist or delivery correspondence.
7. Human reviewers decide whether the bundle is complete, legally current, and suitable for transmission.
