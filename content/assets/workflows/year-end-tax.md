# Year-end corporate tax workflow

1. Identify the final financial statements and trial-balance exports supplied by the company.
2. Build a tax-adjustment register with evidence and legal basis for every adjustment.
3. Evaluate knowledge-based, technology-park, and R&D claims separately.
4. Complete the corporate-tax task template.
5. Run deterministic calculations when available.
6. Produce the draft return, reconciliation, open-questions list, and evidence index.
