# Tax defense workflow

1. Record the notice, service date, deadline, disputed issues, and amounts.
2. Preserve the original assessment and all filing receipts.
3. Create an issue matrix: authority position, company response, legal basis, evidence, and status.
4. Distinguish facts, legal interpretation, assumptions, and missing evidence.
5. Draft the objection or defense without inventing facts or citations.
6. Require human legal/tax review before filing.
